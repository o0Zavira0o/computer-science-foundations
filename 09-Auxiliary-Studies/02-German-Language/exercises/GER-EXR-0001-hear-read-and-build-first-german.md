## Task

Use an authoritative learner-audio source for German and work with these expressions:

```text
Guten Morgen.
Guten Tag.
Hallo.
Tschüss.
Ich heiße …
Wie heißt du?
Wie heißen Sie?
Danke.
Bitte.
```

For each expression, create a four-column record:

1. exact German spelling;
2. what you actually hear (notes in plain language are fine; do not invent fake IPA);
3. communicative function;
4. one situation in which you would use it.

Then record yourself performing one informal and one polite name exchange. Listen back once for rhythm/stress and once for the consonant/vowel features you noticed.

## Evidence of success

You can produce both exchanges without reading, you preserve `du` versus `Sie`, you can type `ä/ö/ü/ß` correctly when needed, and at least five expressions have a sound observation based on actual audio rather than English spelling intuition.

## Hints

If a sound is difficult, shorten the task: isolate the syllable, imitate it, then rebuild the word and phrase. Accuracy improves more reliably from attentive repetition than from saying the whole sentence faster.
