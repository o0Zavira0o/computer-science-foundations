# Neurotechnology & Neural Engineering

Track ID: `neurotechnology-neural-engineering`
Code: `NNE`
Status: active

This Auxiliary Studies track starts from zero subject-specific knowledge and develops a systems-level understanding of how nervous-system activity can be measured, interpreted, modulated, restored, and interfaced with engineered devices.

It deliberately treats neurotechnology as more than brain-computer interfaces. The curriculum integrates neuroscience, neural recording, instrumentation, signal processing, decoding and encoding, stimulation, neuroprosthetics, implant engineering, tissue response, neuroimaging, clinical translation, regulation, human factors, cybersecurity, and neuroethics.

## Safety and scope

This is an educational engineering track, not a guide for self-experimentation, diagnosis, treatment, surgery, implantation, or unsupervised neural stimulation. Clinical and experimental procedures require appropriate professional, institutional, regulatory, and ethical oversight.
