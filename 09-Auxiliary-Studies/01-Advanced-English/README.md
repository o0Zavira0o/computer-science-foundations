# Advanced English

**Track ID:** `advanced-english`  
**Code:** `AEN`  
**Status:** scaffolded

Post-C2 English for learners who already command general English at CEFR C2: precision, register, rhetoric, stylistics, advanced writing, literature, literary criticism, linguistics where useful, and high-level academic/professional expression.
